About these estimates

TrustGov scores are posterior means from a dynamic comparative public opinion (DCPO) latent-variable model, on a 0–1 scale where higher values indicate greater public trust in the national government. Because they are model estimates rather than direct survey percentages, each country-year carries measurement uncertainty — the sd, q10, and q90 columns. Analyses that use these scores should propagate that uncertainty rather than treating the means as known quantities.

Coverage is uneven: cases surveyed more often have more precise estimates and longer series. Wide bands mean sparse underlying survey data, not volatile opinion.
